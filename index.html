I'm Devansh Mishra, a software engineer obsessed with clean architecture, scalable systems, and the pursuit of digital craftsmanship.

My journey in software engineering is driven by a deep-seated curiosity to understand how complex systems operate under the hood. I specialize in backend architecture, focusing on distributed systems, microservices, and performance optimization. For me, code is not just instructions for a machine; it's a medium for solving real-world problems with elegance and efficiency.

Over the years, I've had the privilege of working alongside brilliant minds, tackling challenges that range from optimizing database queries to designing fault-tolerant architectures capable of handling massive scale. I believe in the philosophy of "quiet luxury" in code—where the complexity is hidden beneath a simple, intuitive interface, and the true value shines through reliability and speed.

When I'm not deep in an IDE, you can find me exploring the latest advancements in cloud computing, contributing to open-source, or refining my understanding of system design patterns. I approach every project with a meticulous attention to detail, ensuring that the foundation is robust enough to support future growth.
